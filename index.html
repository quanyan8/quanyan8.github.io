Quanyan Zhu

Associate Professor, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering
New York University Tandon School of Engineering

I study how strategic agents (people, machines, and now AI agents) make decisions, compete, and cooperate, and how to design systems that stay secure and resilient when some of them are adversarial. My work combines game theory, control theory, and learning theory to address problems in cybersecurity, cyber-physical systems, and agentic AI.

I also direct the Laboratory for Agile and Resilient Complex Systems (LARX) and hold an affiliated appointment at NYU's Center for Urban Science and Progress (CUSP).

Research

Agentic AI and LLM multi-agent systems

Game-theoretic foundations for coordination, competition, and incentives among AI agents; reliable agentic workflows; the economics of agents and tokens; insurance for agentic AI.

Dynamic games and multi-agent learning

Strategic learning, conjectural and Berk–Nash equilibria, mechanism design, and coalition formation in large-scale socio-technical systems.

Cyber resilience and deception

Defensive deception, moving target defense, automated penetration testing, and AI-enabled autonomous cyber defense.

Cognitive security and human factors

Human vulnerabilities, cognitive biases, and misinformation as attack surfaces, and system-scientific defenses that account for them.

Cyber-physical-human systems

Secure and resilient control of critical infrastructure, IoT, robotics, 5G networks, and cyber manufacturing.

Risk, insurance, and supply chains

Cyber insurance, risk preference design, and IoT supply chain risk analysis and mitigation.

Books

New manuscripts: sample chapters

Agentic AI: Foundations, Systems, and Security Quanyan Zhu · manuscript, 2026

A graduate textbook on agents that plan, call tools, and act with delegated authority, built on decision theory, games, and learning. Three parts cover agents as decision-makers, building agentic systems, and security, resilience, and governance.

Sample: preface, full contents, and Part I (Chapters 1–3), 126 pages.

Sample chapters (PDF, 1.4 MB)
Agentic Learning: Game-Theoretic and AI Foundations Tao Li and Quanyan Zhu · Birkhäuser, forthcoming

How agents learn in strategic environments: behavioral, cognitive, and epistemic learning, from equilibrium and non-equilibrium dynamics to world models and conjectural learning. Fifteen chapters in five parts, about 570 pages.

Sample: contents, notation, and Chapters 1–3, 121 pages.

Sample chapters (PDF, 3.2 MB)
AI Tokenomics: Production, Pricing, and Value in Token-Metered Intelligence Quanyan Zhu · manuscript, 2026

The economics of the token as the unit through which machine intelligence is produced, metered, priced, and governed. Twenty-one chapters and four appendices, about 710 pages.

Sample: preface, full contents, and Part I (Chapters 1–3), 103 pages.
